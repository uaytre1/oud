    Before you start
    • Strings are numbered from the thinnest. String 1 is the thinnest one, nearest the floor when you hold the oud. The drawing shows it at the bottom.
    • Fingers of your left hand: 1 = index, 2 = middle, 3 = ring, 4 = pinky. 0 / open = play the string with no finger.
    • The oud has no frets: put the finger down, listen, and slide a little until it sounds right. The coach tells you which way to move.
    • Tune first, so the coach hears the right notes.
