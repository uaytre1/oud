How to read this

Strings are numbered from the thinnest: 1 = دو, 2 = صول, 3 = ري, 4 = لا, 6 = low فا. Finger 1 = index, 3 = ring. No finger means the open string. A gold border means hold that note longer. A small dot marks a very quick in-between note.
